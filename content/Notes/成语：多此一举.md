# \[test note]

**Pinyin:** duō cǐ yī jǔ\
**Translation:** “to do something unnecessary; to make a superfluous move.” Literally, “to make one extra move.” Common in spoken and written Chinese, often with mild criticism: the extra action adds nothing useful.

**Usage example:**

> 他已经把问题说清楚了，你又解释了一遍，真是多此一举。\
> Tā yǐjing bǎ wèntí shuō qīngchǔ le, nǐ yòu jiěshì le yí biàn, zhēnshi duō cǐ yī jǔ.\
> “He had already explained the issue clearly, so explaining it again was completely unnecessary.”
